Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy covers the Mindful Me: Daily Affirmations iOS app (App Store ID 6758354591, “the App”) in Part A, and this website (https://wearefaces.github.io/mindfulme/) in Part B.

Part A: The Mindful Me app

A1. The short version

  • No account and no servers of our own. You never sign in, and we do not run a backend or database for the App.
  • Your profile stays on your phone. Your onboarding answers, favourites, the affirmations you write and your stats are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • Some technical data leaves your device. It goes to Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics), Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and OneSignal (push notifications), and the App may download fonts from Google Fonts. This data is tied to random device or app-installation identifiers, not to your name.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking permission. The App does not show ads, does not include an advertising SDK and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. Without that permission, iOS does not give the App your advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

The App stores the following in standard iOS app storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to any third party.

Sensitive answers. Religious preference is a special category of personal data under the GDPR. The App keeps it on your device only. It is not sent to us or to our service providers, and it is not included in analytics, crash reports or push notifications.

Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot see, export, correct or recover it. You control it:

  • To change your answers, redo onboarding from Settings. This clears your saved preferences.
  • To clear favourites and custom affirmations, reset affirmations in Settings.
  • To delete everything, delete the App. This removes all of the App's data from your device.

Depending on your Apple settings, app data may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

The App's own code sends no data anywhere. Your name, onboarding answers, favourites and custom affirmations are not passed to any of these services. We do not set user IDs, user properties, custom events, tags or crash keys that could contain them.

About Google. Firebase Analytics is a Google service, and the version in the App includes Google's standard advertising-identifier support. The App never asks for tracking permission, so that support cannot read your IDFA. Google processes Firebase data under its own terms and policies (see A6).

Objecting to analytics. You can object to analytics at any time by emailing us at neo.goldman@mail.grokbot.com. The App does not yet have an in-app switch for analytics. Because the data is tied to a random identifier rather than to you, the only way to stop analytics collection completely on your device today is to delete the App. We will also consider your objection when deciding how we use analytics in future versions.

Push notifications. The App asks for notification permission so it can send affirmation reminders, which are scheduled locally on your device. If you allow notifications, OneSignal also lets us send notifications from its dashboard, for example about new content or app updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Mindful Me.

Sharing. When you share an affirmation through the share sheet, Messages, WhatsApp or Instagram, the image and text go to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies. We do not receive a copy. To prepare an Instagram post, the App saves the image to your Photos and puts it on the clipboard for 5 minutes.

A5. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or include advertising SDKs, and we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission or access your IDFA.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, health data or precise location.
  • We do not read your photo library. The App only adds images to Photos when you choose to save or share one.

A8. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you reset it in the App or delete the App.
  • Firebase Analytics: event-level data is deleted automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics, which is at most 14 months. Aggregated reports that do not identify a device may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and the identifiers linked to them are kept for 90 days.
  • OneSignal: the push subscription record is kept while it exists in our OneSignal account. We delete it on request (see A10).
  • Google Fonts: see Google's Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

A9. Children and teenagers

The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and has no age check. During onboarding it asks for an age range, and one of the options is “13 to 17”. That answer stays on your device and is never sent to us. The technical data in A4 is the same for every user and is not linked to age. If you are a parent or guardian and want us to delete data about your child, contact us at neo.goldman@mail.grokbot.com.

A10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interests (including analytics), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example, by turning off notifications). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

We have no account system, so we usually cannot link the data held by Firebase or OneSignal to you as a person (GDPR Article 11). If you want us to act on that data, email us. If you can, include the device's Firebase installation ID or OneSignal ID, and we will do what we can. Data stored only on your device is already fully under your control (see A3).

We answer requests within one month. Email: neo.goldman@mail.grokbot.com.

Complaints. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), https://www.imy.se, or to the data protection authority where you live or work.
